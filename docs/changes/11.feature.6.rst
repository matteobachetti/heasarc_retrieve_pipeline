Events are barycentred with the ``barycenter`` package by default for NuSTAR, NICER, RXTE, XMM-Newton (from the PPS orbit file) and Chandra (on DE430). HEASOFT's ``barycorr`` (NuSTAR, NICER, RXTE), SAS's ``barycen`` (XMM-Newton) and CIAO's ``axbary`` (Chandra, on DE405) stay available as configurable fallbacks. The correction is applied at the position that was searched, not at the detected or header position. ``barycenter_file`` also runs outside a Prefect flow and skips finished work without HEASOFT. ``tools/barycenter_validation/`` compares the engines on one real reduction per mission.
