XMM-Newton is supported. EPIC pn, MOS1 and MOS2 exposures are reduced independently (one failure does not lose the rest; ``config['cameras']`` restricts the cameras), by the PPS route when the archive has PPS products and by an ODF route (``epproc``/``emproc``) otherwise, chosen by listing the archive. Flares are cut at the threshold PPS chose, imaging and timing-mode events are cleaned and screened, the extraction window is measured on the cleaned events, pile-up is measured with ``epatplot`` and reported without correcting it, and spectra are extracted with ``especget``. Only the files the chosen route reads are downloaded. Products are named ``xmm<OBSID>_pn_S003_imaging_src.evt`` and similar; ``tools/rename_xmm_outputs.py`` migrates older reductions. Needs ESA's SAS in the environment.
