Chandra ACIS and HRC observations are supported, through CIAO. The detector is read from ``SIM_Z``, the observation from the archive's level-2 products, and the sky pixel scale from the event list. Extraction regions are sized from the PSF at the source's off-axis angle, background flares are screened, ACIS pile-up is measured with ``pileup_map`` and reported, ACIS spectra and grating products are extracted, and ``products="repro"`` re-runs ``chandra_repro``. A warning is given when another source sits in the background ring. Diagnostics and report pages cover Chandra too.
